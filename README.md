# CMP_SCI-4700P-Computer-Forensics-Final

Scenario

Dolos Financial Services is a small stock brokerage based in St. Louis. Being a small firm, Dolos has limited network monitoring or other security tools, and employees have a fair amount of freedom to do their jobs as they see fit.

The firm has become concerned about the activities of a financial analyst named Christopher Gardner, who has just resigned from Dolos. Gardner’s manager, Kurt Aubuchon, doesn’t have any specific suspicions, but he believes that Gardner had been acting strangely before his resignation. Aubuchon is concerned that Gardner may have been removing Dolos proprietary documentation from the network prior to his resignation. In addition, one of Gardner’s coworkers told Aubuchon that she had overheard Gardner having a hushed phone call with someone he called “Sharon” or “Cherie”. The coworker thought Gardner said something about starting his own financial firm. Dolos does employee a woman named Sharron Hammond, but she has not resigned, and no one has said she’s been acting strangely.

When Aubuchon went through Gardner’s desk after Gardner’s departure, he found a 16GB USB drive that Gardner had apparently left behind. USB drives are typically prohibited at Dolos, although enforcement of this rule is lax. Aubuchon is concerned that Gardner may have copied Dolos data to the USB drive, intending to take it with him.

Aubuchon wants to determine if Gardner had copied Dolos data to the drive, or if there is any other evidence on the drive that would suggest Gardner had been trying to remove data from the Dolos network. Aubuchon is especially interested in financial documents and customer information. Aubuchon would also be interested in any evidence that might indicate that Gardner was working with another person, either inside Dolos or outside, and that they were planning to start a rival financial firm.

You are a forensic examiner working for a private investigative firm, Apex Forensics. Aubuchon has engaged Apex to conduct a thorough forensic examination. Other forensic examiners at Apex are conducting examinations of Dolos severs and Gardner’s workstation. You are assigned to examine the USB drive. Your goal is to determine whether there is any evidence on the drive that would indicate that Gardner was mishandling Dolos data, removing it from the network, and/or working with another individual to start his own financial firm.
